## About

I'm a full-stack developer from the Gold Coast, Australia, with a BSc in Computer
Science and a specialisation in Cloud Technologies and DevOps. I've designed and
shipped numerous production websites for businesses and private clients, from
booking platforms and business sites to AI assistants and browser games. My
strength is connecting both sides of a project: polished, responsive interfaces in
front, with secure APIs, organised data, and reliable cloud infrastructure behind
them.

This repository is the source for my personal portfolio site.

## Highlights

- **Minimal dark design** with an animated accent and a single, focused layout.
- **Floating pill navigation** that condenses on scroll and tracks the active
  section, with a bottom tab bar on mobile.
- **Animated tech marquee** showing the stack I work with.
- **Interactive cards** with a cursor-tracking border glow.
- **Project carousel** that pages through my work four at a time, with autoplay,
  dot indicators, and touch swipe.
- **Fully responsive** and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
